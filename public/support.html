Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase tickets?

Browse events on our homepage, select your desired event, choose your ticket type and quantity, and complete the checkout process.

Can I get a refund?

Refund policies vary by event. Please check the specific event details or contact our support team for assistance.

How do I access my tickets?

After purchase, you'll receive a confirmation email with your tickets and QR codes. You can also access them through your account.
