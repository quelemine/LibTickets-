About LibTickets

Liberia's premier event ticketing platform, connecting people to unforgettable experiences across the nation.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, LibTickets was born from a simple vision: to make event discovery and ticket booking accessible, seamless, and enjoyable for everyone in Liberia. We recognized the need for a modern, reliable platform that could bring together event organizers and attendees in one unified space.

What started as a small idea has grown into Liberia's leading event ticketing platform, serving thousands of users across all 15 counties. From concerts in Monrovia to sports events in Gbarnga, we're proud to be part of every memorable moment.

??

Our Mission

To revolutionize how Liberians experience events by providing a secure, user-friendly platform that connects communities through shared experiences. We believe that every event, big or small, deserves to be discovered and enjoyed.

We're committed to supporting local organizers, promoting cultural events, and making entertainment accessible to everyone, regardless of location or budget.
