Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view my tickets?

Go to the "My Tickets" section in your dashboard to view all your purchased tickets with QR codes.

Can I cancel my booking?

Bookings can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the event. Contact our support team for assistance.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. Please contact our support team to initiate a refund request.
