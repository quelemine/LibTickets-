Where We Operate

LibTickets brings events to major cities across Liberia. Find events near you and discover what's happening in your area.

Monrovia

Capital City • Montserrado County

As Liberia's capital and largest city, Monrovia hosts the majority of our events including concerts, conferences, and sports competitions at venues like the Samuel Kanyon Doe Sports Complex and various hotels and conference centers.

Paynesville

Largest City • Montserrado County

Paynesville is a rapidly growing city with a vibrant community. We host numerous local events, community gatherings, and entertainment shows in this bustling area.

Gbarnga

Central Region • Bong County

Gbarnga serves as a key regional hub in central Liberia. Our events here include cultural festivals, educational conferences, and sports tournaments that bring together communities from across the region.
