Help Center

Find answers to common questions and get help with your tickets

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign up" in the top right corner of our website. Fill in your name, email, and password to create your account.

How do I find events?

Browse our homepage for featured events, or visit the Concerts, Sports, or Conferences pages to filter by category.

How do I purchase tickets?

Select an event, choose your ticket type and quantity, and proceed to checkout. You can pay using LRD or USD.

Ticket Management

Where can I find my purchased tickets?

Your tickets are sent to your email after purchase. You can also view them in your account dashboard.

How do I use my ticket at an event?

Show your QR code at the event entrance. Our staff will scan it to validate your ticket.

Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Tickets are non-transferable unless specified by the event organizer. Contact support for special requests.

Payments & Refunds

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept mobile money, bank transfers, and cash payments at designated locations.

What is your refund policy?

Refund policies vary by event. Please check the event details or contact support for specific refund information.

How do currency conversions work?

We display prices in both LRD and USD. The exchange rate is set by the platform and updated regularly.

Account Issues

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click "Log in" and select "Forgot password" to reset your password via email.

How do I update my profile information?

Log in to your account and navigate to the profile section to update your personal information.

Still Need Help?

If you couldn't find the answer to your question, please contact our support team.
